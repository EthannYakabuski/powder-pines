POWDER PINES — IMPLEMENTED GAME DESIGN

Godot 4.5.1, Compatibility renderer, portrait 540 x 960 logical viewport.
Android is primary. Mouse/keyboard and Web remain playable without native services.

Mountain loop: open and groom twelve named runs across Sunny Front and North Face,
pan the 1,700-unit hill, watch moving skier NPCs and a gondola, earn coins, upgrade
the village, and return to the slopes for a relaxed skill-based slalom.

Village: eight chalet tiers, twenty staff slots, eight ticket/rental desk tiers.
Every upgrade affects income. Offline income earns 50% of the current rate,
capped at four hours plus two hours per chalet upgrade, to a maximum of eighteen.

Slalom: touch-drag or hold the mouse to steer; arrow keys also work. Twelve gates
use deterministic alternating centers across a roughly 28-second course. A missed
gate breaks the combo but does not end the outing. Coins and XP are granted once
at finish; leaving early grants nothing. The normal prize is never contingent on
an ad. An earned-reward callback may double a finished outing's coin prize once.

Seasons: open 4, then 7, then 10, then all 12 runs (capped at 12), earn the required
season coins, and finish three outings to advance. New seasons preserve the
mountain, add 20% income per season relative to the initial factor, grant coins
and XP, and reset only the season-specific milestones. Progress can continue
after season three.

Retention: seven consecutive UTC-day login gifts (80 to 500 coins), daily income,
gates and outing quests, player levels with coin grants, twenty local achievements
and four leaderboard metrics. Missing a day restarts the gift streak without
removing village progress. Device-clock manipulation is not server-verified.

Platform integration contract: /root/Services provides banner visibility,
round-finished interstitial cadence, earned-reward callbacks, Play Games sign-in,
achievement unlocking, score submission and privacy options. Missing services
disable ad reward buttons and preserve offline gameplay. Root owns native plugin
configuration, real-ID placeholders, exports, CI and publication handoff.

Art/audio: supplied Kenney Tiny Ski packed tiles and UI click audio, with their
original CC0 notices copied alongside the assets. An original low-volume alpine
bell loop accompanies the game and can be muted using the header music button.

Possible future DLC: Night Lights adds a separate moonlit mountain, lantern
slalom and cozy cabin furnishings without changing the first game's economy.

Capture arguments (after --): --capture=resort|runs|lodge|season|daily|journal|slalom
--capture-path=C:/absolute/path.png. Optional --test-profile uses an explicit
local QA profile with unlocked runs for illustrative QA captures; it is not a
production player statistic or evidence of live service verification.
